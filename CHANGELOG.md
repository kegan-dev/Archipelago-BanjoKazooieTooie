# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (alpha)

The first public release, for testing.

- Banjo-Kazooie and Banjo-Tooie in one slot, on one 64 MiB ROM the client makes from your own two ROMs.
- The entrance to Gruntilda's Lair in Tooie's Spiral Mountain leads into Banjo-Kazooie's Lair, and back; each Tooie
  save file has its own Banjo-Kazooie save.
- Shared moves: 17 of Banjo-Kazooie's moves are Tooie's items, learned in both games, progressive ones included;
  `randomize_bk_moves` sets the moves you start with in both.
- `goal`: Tooie's, Banjo-Kazooie's (Gruntilda beaten), or both.
- `cross_game_world_entrances`: the two games' world entrances shuffled together.
- Traps, egg and feather nests, death link and tag link go to the game you're playing.
- Cheato gives his rewards in King Jingaling's throne room.
- Installs alone: carries the Banjo-Kazooie world and Banjo-Tooie AP V4.13.1.
- Played on Project64 3.0.1 and ares v148.
